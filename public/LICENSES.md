## Quote texts

120 short excerpts (20 per category), checked against the source texts below. All authors and translators died before 1955. These editions are listed as public domain by Project Gutenberg; only the original works are excerpted, not modern introductions or editorial notes. Spacing and footnote markers are normalized; an ellipsis marks an excerpt that ends mid-sentence. Source titles are retained with each quote. No song lyrics or modern quote collections are included.

## Generated alert WAVs

`alerts/{signature,bell,chime,digital,wood,none}-{focus-done,break-over}.wav`
are original synthesized audio, dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Their source is
`src/audio/alertSynth.ts`; `scripts/gen-alerts.mts` regenerates them during every
build. No third-party recordings are used. The `none` files contain silence;
native alarms skip playback for that choice.

Every audible pack has a distinct pitch pattern for each event. Digital retains
its three notes and two rests in both variants. Regenerated WAVs use the same CC0
source and dedication above. Timer ticks
(soft clock, wood and digital) are also original procedural synthesis, with no
third-party samples.

The Kitestring kite and curved line are original artwork made for this project.
Source masters: `brand/app.svg`, `brand/favicon.svg`, `brand/tray.svg`.
This icon family and its generated assets use the same license as the project.
No third-party artwork is used.

- App and DMG: `desktop/build/icon.png` and `icon.icns`, from `brand/app.svg`.
- macOS Icon Composer: `desktop/build/Kitestring.icon/icon.json`, with solid
  `kite-left.svg` (#F29E7C), `kite-right.svg` (#F7CF7E) and `line.svg` (#E7E2FF)
  layers. The existing gradient background remains in icon.json.
- Menu bar: transparent black 18/36px tray templates from `brand/tray.svg`.
- Web: `icon.svg`, 192/512px PNGs and Apple touch icon from `brand/app.svg`;
  padded `icon-maskable-512.png` has an opaque background and a safe-zone mark.
  SVG/ICO/32px favicons come from `brand/favicon.svg`.
- Share card: `public/og.jpg`, generated from `design/og/og-card.svg` with the
  same original kite mark, name and tagline.

Regenerate with `npm --prefix desktop run build:icon` (librsvg, ImageMagick,
macOS iconutil). The builder reads only tracked masters in `brand/`.
